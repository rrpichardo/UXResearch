Movie and TV Show Recommendations: Instructions

Welcome

Thank you for participating in this study. Our goal is to understand how individuals use an AI chatbot to discover movies and TV shows. There are no correct or incorrect responses. This study evaluates the chatbot, not the participants.

This study has three parts: a short questionnaire, a chat with the AI recommendation chatbot, and another short questionnaire at the end.

Scenario
Picture yourself choosing something to watch on Netflix, but you don't have a specific title in mind. Instead of browsing the homepage, you'll use an AI chatbot to help you find a movie or TV show. Think about your own interests and preferences, just like you normally would.
Your Task

Pick one movie or TV show that you would actually want to watch. Focus on your own tastes and preferences, not what you think others might pick.

Participant Instructions
  1. Complete the initial questionnaire.
    Before you use the chatbot, answer a few questions about how familiar you are with movies and TV shows, and what you usually look for when choosing something to watch.
  2. Begin chatting with the AI recommendation chatbot.
    In your first message, let the chatbot know what you're looking for. You can mention your favorite genre, your mood, or something you've liked before. Use your own words and preferences.
  3. Respond to any questions the chatbot asks.
    If the chatbot asks for more details, answer based on what you would actually want to watch.
  4. Review the recommendations.
    Look over the information the chatbot gives you and decide which title you might want to watch. For each suggestion, you'll see the title, how long the movie is or how many seasons the series has, and a short description.
  5. Choose one title from the chatbot's recommendations.
    Pick the one you're most likely to watch next.
  6. Complete the final questionnaire.
    Once you've looked at the recommendations and made your choice, answer a few questions about your experience with the chatbot. Please base your answers on the conversation you just had.

There are no right or wrong choices when it comes to what you like to watch. We're interested in your experience, so please answer honestly.
