Movie and TV Show Recommendations: Instructions

Reminder: You're choosing something to watch on Netflix. Pick one movie or TV show you would actually want to watch, based on your own tastes.

  1. Begin chatting with the AI recommendation chatbot.
    In your first message, let the chatbot know what you're looking for. You can mention your favorite genre, your mood, or something you've liked before.
  2. Respond to any questions the chatbot asks.
    If it asks for more details, answer based on what you would actually want to watch.
  3. Review the recommendations.
    For each suggestion, you'll see the title, how long the movie is or how many seasons the series has, and a short description.
  4. Choose one title from the chatbot's recommendations.
    Pick the one you're most likely to watch next, and remember its title. You'll be asked for it in the final questionnaire.
  5. Click "End Chat" when you've made your choice.
    This takes you to the final questionnaire.

There are no right or wrong choices when it comes to what you like to watch. We're interested in your experience, so please answer honestly.
